# MarketScout FAQ

### Why use MarketScout instead of just using Inara?

MarketScout and Inara solve different problems.

Inara is excellent for checking public market data and finding trade routes from information uploaded by commanders. The challenge is that the best deals can disappear quickly once they become public, especially when supply is limited.

MarketScout is built for a different moment in the trading loop: scouting. It helps you review the market data you personally collect while flying, so you can spot useful prices before they are widely known or before they appear in public trade tools.

I personally run EDMC with EDDN enabled most of the time, and I encourage others to do the same. Community tools like Inara, Spansh, EDSM, and others depend on commanders sharing data.

That said, I also understand the practical reality of trading communities: sometimes a commander may want to keep a newly found trade opportunity private for their group for a few hours. MarketScout includes an EDDN station-data indicator in the top status bar so you can see whether EDMC is currently sending station data to EDDN without opening EDMC settings every time.

On compatible EDMC forks, the status bar can also show the types of delayed station messages that have not been sent yet and let you discard those queued messages. This control is only available when the running EDMC build exposes the required hook; it does not change the upload settings in standard EDMC.
